Safety Centre

Simple rules that keep every book deal safe
🛡️
Your phone number stays private by default

Buyers reach you through in-app chat or your Telegram handle. A number is only ever shown if you personally switch the contact preference to WhatsApp / Call.

Before you pay
  • See the book first. Meet in a public place — a college gate, a library, a busy café.
  • Check the condition against the photos and the description before handing over money.
  • Pay in person once you have the book in your hands. Never pay a stranger in advance.
  • Take a friend for late-evening pickups.
Red flags to walk away from
  • Asking for a UPI transfer, gift card or “token advance” before you see the book.
  • Refusing to meet in person or to share a real photo of the book.
  • Pushing you to move the chat off BookBridge immediately.
  • A price that is suspiciously low for a popular title.
  • Any request for OTPs, bank details or screen sharing.
Report a listing

Every listing has a Report button. Reports go straight to our moderation queue with the reason and your note, and the listing can be hidden within minutes. The seller is never told who reported them.

Block someone

Blocked people disappear from your feed, and they cannot message you again. They are not notified. You can unblock anytime.

Ratings you can trust

Only buyers who actually chatted with a seller can rate them, so every review comes from a real conversation. Sellers with 3+ reviews and a 4.5★ average earn the Top seller badge; email-confirmed accounts show a Verified badge.

What BookBridge never asks for
  • Your password — nobody from our team will ever ask.
  • OTPs or bank / card details.
  • Remote access to your phone or laptop.

Spot something suspicious? Report the listing and we will review it.
